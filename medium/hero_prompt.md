# Hero image prompt (abstract, light theme)

Use with any image model at 16:9 (e.g., 2400 x 1350). Medium crops the hero to about 2:1 in feeds, so keep the important shapes in the middle band.

```
Abstract editorial illustration on a warm off-white background (#FCFCFB), light theme, lots of empty space.
Two thin lines start from the same small dot on the left side.
The first line is warm orange (#EB6834). It runs straight and short and stops at a small open circle a third of the way across.
The second line is calm blue (#2A78D6). It travels further, curving gently through four or five tiny nodes, and ends at a slightly larger filled circle on the right.
Behind both lines, a very faint grid of small light-grey dots suggests a table of data. A few dots near the blue path are slightly darker, as if they were checked.
Flat vector style, precise thin strokes, soft shadows only, no gradients, no texture noise.
No text, no numbers, no letters, no logos, no people, no brains, no lightbulbs, no robots.
Calm, minimal, Swiss editorial look.
```

Negative prompt (if the model supports one): text, letters, watermark, logo, dark background, neon, glow, 3D render, brain, robot, lightning bolt, clock.
